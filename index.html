Laboratory of Cell Behavior

Our goal is to understand how cells regulate their behavior in health and disease.

We focus on some of the most dramatic cell behaviors – cell death, migration, and engulfment (phagocytosis). These behaviors are essential for normal development and tissue homeostasis, and they go awry in many diseases.

Approach

From a living organism to human disease

We study single cells within the physiological context of a living, multicellular organism, and translate our findings to human disease. To do this, we take a multi-system approach that combines work in C. elegans, cultured cells, and human tissues.
